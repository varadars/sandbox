Tools & Process

I have a Canon EOS M50, but none of the pictures on this site were taken with that camera; most of them are just from my phone, which is a Google Pixel 10. One thing I like to do when taking photos, which is probably a remnant from a random photography class I took, is to turn the grid on and move subjects into the intersections of the grid where possible. I also turn down the brightness and select less bright areas or select the brightest area depending on the brightness levels I want in my photo.
